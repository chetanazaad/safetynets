Rooftop Safety Solutions

From residential terraces to commercial rooftops and sports enclosures, we provide comprehensive heavy-duty safety net installations built to last under all weather conditions.

Rooftop Safety Netting

Open rooftops, terraces, and courtyards are highly functional spaces but pose extreme safety risks. For residential properties, they are playing areas for children and pets. For commercial buildings, they can be recreation lounges, storage yards, or high-risk maintenance areas. Our rooftop safety solutions create a secure structural ceiling or vertical perimeter wall, enclosing open-air locations safely without blocking breeze, sunlight, or skies.

Common Use Cases

Our industrial-grade netting configurations support multiple residential and commercial applications:

  • Children's Rooftop Play Zones: Creating safe high-tensile enclosures so children can run and play sports safely.
  • Elderly Walkway Protection: Adding fall-preventive barriers on open-air terrace walk paths.
  • Rooftop Sports Nets: Installing boundary nets for terrace cricket pitches, football play courts, or basketball screens, restricting ball damage.
  • Courtyard & Duct Closures: Preventing pigeons and monkeys from descending into building courtyards and open ducts.

Technical Specifications

Specification Details
Material Composition Heavy-Duty High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) - UV Treated
Twine Thickness 1.5mm to 3.0mm (multi-braided high impact cords)
Mesh Dimensions 40mm x 40mm (ball stop/pet proof) to 75mm x 75mm (standard safety)
Border Rope 8mm to 12mm thick polypropylene ropes for tensioned border frames
Impact Capacity Withstands distributed weights up to 250 kg
Support Cables Galvanized Iron (GI) or Stainless Steel cables with tight turnbuckles

Warranty & Professional Fastening

Because rooftops endure the highest sun, rain, and wind exposure, we configure our nets with double UV stabilization compounds and heavy-duty anchors. We provide a **3-year comprehensive warranty** on all installations. Our specialized engineers use clean scaffolding and structural supports to secure all netting borders safely.
