Child Safety Nets

Our ultra-strong, certified child safety nets are installed by professionals to create a secure, toddler-proof barrier on balconies, open stairways, and high-rise windows.

Why Child Safety Nets Matter

Balconies and windows in modern apartments offer fresh air but pose high accidental fall risks for curious children and pets. Our child safety nets provide a robust, non-intrusive safety barrier that allows you to leave doors and windows open with total peace of mind. Engineered using high-density polyethylene (HDPE), these nets are designed to absorb heavy impacts without tearing or breaking away from their structural hooks.

Net Types & Materials

We supply two core types of safety netting configured according to your property requirements:

  • Monofilament Copolymer Nets: High-transparency, nylon-like feel that offers maximum safety with minimal visual intrusion.
  • Braided HDPE Safety Nets: Thick, highly visible co-polymer nets ideal for heavy-duty protection in open terraces and balconies.

Technical Specifications

Specification Details
Material 100% Virgin UV-Stabilized Nylon / HDPE Copolymer
Twine Thickness 0.7mm (Monofilament) to 2.5mm (Braided)
Mesh Dimensions 25mm x 25mm (pet safe) to 40mm x 40mm (standard child safe)
Breaking Strength Individually tested up to 150 kg per square meter
Knot System Double-knotted locked grid nodes to prevent mesh widening
Fasteners Used Stainless steel hook screws anchors spaced every 6 inches

Warranty & Lifespan

All child safety nets are treated with direct UV-block inhibitors during extrusion, giving them an active outdoor service lifespan of over 5 to 7 years. We provide a **3-year comprehensive warranty** on all installations, covering mesh breakage, knot loosening, and wall fastening adjustments.
