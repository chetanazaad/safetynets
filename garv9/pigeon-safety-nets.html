Pigeon Control Nets

Say goodbye to pigeon mess and health hazards. Our UV-stabilized, high-density copolymer nets are 100% bird-proof, durable, and practically invisible from a distance.

The Problem with Pigeons

Pigeons and other birds nesting on balconies and terrace ledges create significant issues. Bird droppings contain highly acidic compounds that ruin paint, corrode metal railings, and deface masonry. More importantly, dried bird droppings can carry up to 60 transmissible diseases, including Histoplasmosis, Psittacosis, and Cryptococcis. Nesting sites also harbor bird mites and fleas, creating severe respiratory risks for children and elderly residents.

Our Premium Solution

We provide chemical-free, harmless, and permanent bird-proofing barriers. Our copolymer safety nets are designed to completely seal balcony openings, duct areas, ventilation shafts, and building gaps, preventing birds from landing or nesting while allowing 100% fresh air and sunlight.
