Born in 2004 in a small studio in Lausanne, AURA was founded on a radical idea: that safety engineering could be practised with the same reverence as perfumery. Both disciplines demand invisible precision. Both protect something irreplaceable. Both, when done well, leave no trace — only the quiet certainty that all is well.

Today, AURA systems protect lives across six continents — in concert halls, offshore platforms, mountain railways, and the world's highest towers.

Composition

How We Distil Safety

Every AURA system is composed in five movements — a process as deliberate and sequential as the crafting of a fine fragrance.

I
Movement One
Listening

We begin not with engineering, but with understanding. Our senior consultants spend time on-site, absorbing the character of the space and the culture of its users before a single calculation is made.

II
Movement Two
Formula

The structural formula is developed in our Lausanne studio — load paths, mesh geometry, attachment philosophy — using proprietary software developed over twenty years of practice.

III
Movement Three
Distillation

Materials are selected with the rigour of a master perfumer choosing ingredients. Each strand, each fitting, each anchor is evaluated not just for strength, but for longevity and invisible harmony with its environment.

IV
Movement Four
Proving

Every system is proof-tested in our ISO 17025 laboratory. The certificate of conformity is not paperwork — it is the signature on a finished composition.

V
Movement Five
Presence

Installation is the moment the formula becomes presence. Our supervised installation teams ensure the system settles into its environment exactly as designed — and then we disappear, leaving only the quiet confidence that the space is safe.
