— Section 02 / Philosophy

The philosophy of Ma.

Ma (間) is the Japanese concept of negative space — the pause between notes, the silence between words, the void between walls. It is not emptiness, but presence. A space with its own weight.

"A safety net is the architecture of Ma.
It holds the void without filling it."

— Section 03 / Materials

Three weaves.

Each material chosen for its strength, its silence, and the way it ages against the architecture.

Nylon

ナイロン

Soft to the touch, elastic under load. The choice for spaces where bodies might fall — staircases, mezzanines, atriums. Holds 250kg without rupture.

HDPE

高密度ポリエチレン

High-density polyethylene. Stiff, weather-resistant, invisible from distance. The choice for exteriors — balconies, façades, roofs. Holds against wind and UV.

Polyester

ポリエステル

Finer than a hair. The choice for the smallest voids — windows, vents, the gaps where insects enter. Woven tight enough to stop a mosquito.

— Section 03 / Atelier

The hand behind the net.

Every Filet net is cut, woven, and edged by a single artisan. We do not subcontract the fitting, we do not automate the weaving. The hand that ties the first knot ties the last.

This is slow work. A single balcony net takes four hours on the bench. A staircase takes a day. An atrium takes a week. We make 240 pieces a year, and we have never made more.
