Savoir-Faire

The Art of Protection

I
Site Analysis & Engineering

Our structural engineers conduct on-site load assessments, wind studies and geometric surveys. Every project begins with understanding its unique demands at a molecular level.

II
Material Selection

We source from certified mills only. Every batch of fibre or alloy undergoes tensile, fatigue and UV-degradation testing before it enters our workshop.

III
Precision Fabrication

CNC knotting machines guided by proprietary software produce consistent tensioning across the full panel. Human artisans inspect every junction by hand before sign-off.

IV
Load Certification

Every assembly is proof-loaded in our ISO 17025-accredited laboratory. Certificates accompany every delivery as standard.

V
Installation & Commissioning

Our supervised installation teams ensure the system performs exactly as modelled. We remain on-call for the lifetime of the installation.
