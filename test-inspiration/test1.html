— Section 03 / Heritage

The craft of protection.

The word kavach comes from Sanskrit — armor, shield, that which guards. For three thousand years, the Indian craft tradition has woven protection: chainmail for warriors, netting for fishermen, mesh for granaries. We continue that tradition in HDPE and nylon, anchored in stainless steel, warranted for fifteen years.

"A net is the quietest form of armor.
It holds without shouting."
