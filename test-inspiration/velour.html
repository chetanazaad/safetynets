The Craft

From Thread to Installation

Five acts of precision that transform raw fibre into a system that carries people's lives.

Act I
The Brief

Every VELOUR engagement begins with a deep listening session. We ask the questions other suppliers don't — about the space, the people, the life of the project after handover.

Act II
The Pattern

Our engineers draft the geometry — load paths, attachment schedules, mesh density maps — in proprietary 3D modelling software developed exclusively for flexible safety systems.

Act III
The Weave

In our fabrication studio, master technicians produce every panel on precision knotting looms. Tension is checked at every junction — not by machine alone, but by trained hands.

Act IV
The Proof

Every system is proof-loaded in our in-house accredited facility. A certificate of conformity accompanies every delivery. No exceptions.

Act V
The Fitting

Like a couturier who attends every fitting, our installation supervisors are present for every deployment. The system is not complete until it performs exactly as modelled.
